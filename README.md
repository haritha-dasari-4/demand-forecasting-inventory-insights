# Demand Forecasting and Inventory Insights

## Project Overview
This project analyzes historical weekly sales data for a Walmart store to understand demand patterns, seasonality, and variability. The objective is to derive actionable insights for inventory planning and demand management.

## Key Insights

1. **Stable long-term demand with short-term fluctuations**  
   Weekly sales vary significantly, but the rolling average shows a stable long-term trend. Inventory planning should rely on smoothed demand rather than reacting to weekly noise.

2. **Clear seasonal and holiday-driven demand patterns**  
   Certain months and holiday periods consistently show higher sales. Inventory levels should be increased ahead of these periods to avoid stockouts.

3. **Moderate demand variability requiring safety stock**  
   Demand fluctuations and occasional spikes indicate the need for safety stock and proactive replenishment to maintain service levels.
